  You are being held close. Cradled like a baby by a gigantic figure in dark robes, held tight to its chest, you cannot see its face. You are not comforted by this action despite the apparent kindness of the gesture. The figure's skin is cold, and its movement is stiff and emotionless.

  A harsh purple light pervades the room, growing in intensity… then you wake up.
