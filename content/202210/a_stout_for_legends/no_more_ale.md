---
title: No More Ale
---

As a dwarf, you are used to free-flowing ale. The failure of these heroes to rescue the brewery is a defeat that you would not have expected. They seemed so capable, but they forgot the most important thing of all.

If the module fails, the tavern no longer has ale on tap. All the dwarves gain the following traits.

**Constantly Complain**: Every time an inconvenience occurs, make sure to mention that it would be much easier to manage if there were some ale to wash it away.

**Degrade the Skills of the Adventurers**: When the adventurers go on an adventure, be sure to mention that they couldn't even save the ale.
