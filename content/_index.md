# NPC Speech

**Take Care of Yourselves:** The most important thing is your health. You are probably not used to this level of activity.

- **Stretch**: Yes, it looks lame. Yes, you should still do it.
- **Drink Water and Eat**: Try to drink a glass of water before you go to a module.
- **Pace Yourself**: A weekend is a long time. There is no shame in taking a break.

**Keep a positive energy in the NPC Shack.**

- Don't talk about the good old days.
- Don't compare yourself to other running teams.
- Take a break or take a nap. If you can't keep your spirits up, excuse yourself.

**We are the face of the event. We hold ourselves to a higher standard.**

- Be respectful to both players and other cast members.
- Don't enable or condone bad behavior. If you see something, say something.
- Focus on the small stuff. Make the small roles sing. Make the ordinary sets special.
- Be dramatic when playing your roles. Don't phone in your performance.

**Tour of the NPC Shack**: Everyone is going to need to know the organization of the NPC Shack and help keep it organized. This organization is a work in progress, and after the event we should discuss any improvement ideas you have. The stuff here was donated, so please be considerate and help make it last.

- **Clock**: We have a large clock set up in the shack. If you can, please wear a watch.
- **Computer**: This is someone's personal computer. Please be kind to it. All the write-ups are on it, as well as a local copy of our website. Verify that it works by printing something.
- **White Headband**: Have one on your person. If you see a player who does not have one, offer them one and replace it when you return to the shack.
- **Pencils**: Carry a golf pencil with you.
- **Costumes**: When you're done, make sure the costume is put away.
- **Belts**: When you are using a tabard, wear a belt.
- **Masks**: When you're done using a mask, wipe it down so it doesn't get anyone sick.
- **Packets**: After fights, don't mix wet packets back in with the fresh packets. It will cause them to decompose.

**Secrecy**: You will learn things while running that are intended to be secret from the players. You will also need to firewall this information in your brain from the knowledge you have while acting as a PC. Sometimes you will need to "play dumb" when acting as a PC.

- Some of what you learn will be common knowledge and will be fine to know as your character. If you have any questions, speak to an event director, and they can help you sort this out.

**Overview of write-ups**: This event may be different from many that you are used to. We have a lot more structure and writing than you have probably seen in the past. This might seem stifling, but we hope that we can convince you that it can make for a great experience for both you and the players.

**Introductions**: Go around in a circle, and have everyone introduce themselves and state the following.

- What is your name?
- What are you excited about regarding the event?
- Which roles do you not want to play?

**Overview of [Campsite]({{< ref "/camps" >}})**: Map, Module Sites, Dangers

**Rewards**: Thank you for doing this. Giving back like this to enable people to have a fun time is a very generous thing to do. We hope that some baubles and tokens will show our gratitude.

- **Goblin Stamps**: You get 100 Goblin Stamps per day.
- **Magic Item Draw**: We will raffle off a magic item. I don't know the specifics of how this works, but I guess it comes from the treasure pool.
