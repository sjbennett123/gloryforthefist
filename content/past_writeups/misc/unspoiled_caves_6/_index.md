Running_Notes: |
  This is one of 10 modules. They will scale with increasing difficulty in terms of what the players will fight, but more importantly, how they will fight. Each cave will have a Bloody Fist Banner at the entrance, just inside the Mod Shack door. The banners are very important to these modules because the Gnawing Ones are afraid of them. They will not advance past a banner or attack you while you brandish one. The players may move the wooden base with the Bloody Fist Banner through the cave to “control” the way the Gnawing Ones engage them. The Gnawing Ones will not leave the caves to pursue the players under any circumstances. The Gnawing Ones have a special way of killing you: they will not drop you and begin a killing blow count like most monsters or players. They want you to be paralyzed and helpless so they can look you in the eyes as they push their claws into your heart and feel the fear gush out onto them while you bleed out on their chitinous exoskeletons.

  The device given to them by Magnus Kane will be a one-time-use “Death Gas” weapon against the Gnawing Ones if they set it off in a cave on Saturday. If anyone remains in the cave one minute after the Fumigation Device is set off, they will be struck with a “Death” effect. The device should be detonated while the players leave the banners in front of the doors to trap the Gnawing Ones in the caves as the fumigation kills them. The gas will remain for one hour after it is released, so the players need to loot the cave before setting off the device, or they will have only 60 seconds to loot and run!
