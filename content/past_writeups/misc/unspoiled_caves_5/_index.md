Running_Notes: |
  This is 1 of 10 modules. They will scale in difficulty, not only in what the players fight but, more importantly, in how they fight. Each cave will have a Bloody Fist Banner at its entrance, just inside the Mod Shack door. The banners are very important to these modules because the Gnawing Ones are afraid of them. They will not advance past a Bloody Fist Banner or attack you while you are brandishing one. The players may move the wooden base with the Bloody Fist Banner through the cave to “control” how the Gnawing Ones engage them. The Gnawing Ones will not leave the caves to pursue the players under any circumstances. The Gnawing Ones have a special way of killing you: they will not drop you and begin a killing blow count as most monsters or players do. They want you to be paralyzed and helpless so they can look you in the eyes as they push their claws into your heart and feel your fear gush onto them while you bleed out on their chitinous exoskeletons.

  The device given to them by Magnus Kane is a one-time-use “Death Gas” weapon against the Gnawing Ones if they set it off in a cave on Saturday. Anyone who remains in the cave one minute after the Fumigation Device is activated will be struck with a “Death” effect. The players should leave the banners in front of the doors to trap the Gnawing Ones in the caves while the fumigation kills them. The gas will remain for 1 hour after it is released, so the players need to loot the cave before activating the device, or they will have only 60 seconds to loot and run!
