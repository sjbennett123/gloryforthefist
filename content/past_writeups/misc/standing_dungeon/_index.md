The Mausoleum of Fallen Heroes in Stonewood is now unlocked as a standing dungeon. This mausoleum has a magical training property that transforms adventurers into the heroes of legend in Stonewood. The Vampire corrupting the top floor was defeated by adventurers, and a Dwarf communed with the ancient hero Feaolin Ironleaf to help decide what to do with the Mayor of Stonewood. At the beginning of the next game, one of the guild masters will introduce the Mausoleum Dungeon and cast Purify Earth on it to cleanse the Green Eyes near the entrance. There are 13 floors of monsters, each with increasing difficulty. Players will be sent here on various quests to retrieve items.

The dungeon will reset one hour after adventurers leave and on each full reset if it is vacant.

Cleared floors will not reset until the dungeon is defeated or adventurers have left the dungeon for one hour. Each floor can have a passageway for plot-related items or side rooms to search for plot purposes.
