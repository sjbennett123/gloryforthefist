Running_Notes: |
  This is 1 of 10 modules. They will scale with increasing difficulty in terms of what they will fight, but more importantly, HOW they will fight. Each Cave will have a Bloody Fist Banner at its entrance, just inside the Mod Shack door. The Banners are VERY important to these modules because the Gnawing Ones are afraid of them. They will not advance past them or attack you if you brandish a Bloody Fist Banner. The players may move the wooden base with the Bloody Fist Banner through the Cave to “control” how the Gnawing Ones engage the Players. The Gnawing Ones will not leave the Caves to pursue the players under any circumstances. The Gnawing Ones have a special way of killing you: they won’t drop you and begin a killing blow count like most monsters or players do. They want you to be paralyzed and helpless so they can look you in the eyes when they push their claws into your heart and feel the fear gush out onto them as you bleed out on their chitinous exoskeletons.

  The Device given to them by Magnus Kane will be a one-time-use “Death Gas” weapon against the Gnawing Ones if they set it off in a Cave on Saturday. If anyone remains in the Cave one minute after the Fumigation Device is set off, they will be struck with a “Death” effect. The device should be detonated after they leave the Banners in front of the Doors to trap the Gnawing Ones in the Caves while the Fumigation kills them. The gas will remain for 1 hour after it is set off, so the players need to loot before activating the device, or they will have only 60 seconds to loot and run!

*Just when you thought the Bloody Fist couldn’t sink any lower. Val’Kaz the Ruthless ordered a creature to destroy the very thing that makes Stonewood so unique: the Stonewood Trees!*

**Unspoiled Cave 1 Notes:** This Cave is the first in the series. The Bloody Rats are skulking around the caves, almost hiding from the Gnawing Ones and looking for some Orc Blood to feed on. The Gnawing Ones will stop creeping around to stalk their prey when they hear the players, and the two creatures will come to an understanding that they are both enemies of the Bloody Fist.

**VAL’KAZ CAN APPEAR IN ANY ONE OF THESE MODULES!!! A DICE ROLL WILL BE MADE AT THE START OF THE EVENT TO DETERMINE WHICH CAVE HE IS IN. HIS GOAL WILL BE TO ESCAPE. HE WILL GAS THE PCS AND CHARGE OUT THE DOOR, LEAVING THE PLAYERS TO FIGHT IN THE CAVE. THIS IS A BIG JUMP SCARE FOR LATER IN THE WEEKEND. IF HE DIES THERE, HE CAN DIE IN THE CAVE.**
