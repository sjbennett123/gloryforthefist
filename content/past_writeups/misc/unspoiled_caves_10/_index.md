Running_Notes: |
  This is one of 10 modules. They will scale with increasing difficulty in terms of what the players will fight, but more importantly, how they will fight. Each cave will have a Bloody Fist Banner at its entrance, just inside the Mod Shack door. The banners are very important to these modules because the Gnawing Ones are afraid of them. They will not advance past them or toward you if you brandish a Bloody Fist Banner. The players may move the wooden base with the Bloody Fist Banner through the cave to “control” the way the Gnawing Ones engage them. The Gnawing Ones will not leave the caves to pursue the players under any circumstances. The Gnawing Ones have a special way of killing you: they will not drop you and begin a killing blow count like most monsters or players. They want you to be paralyzed and helpless so they can look you in the eyes when they push their claws into your heart and feel the fear gush out onto them as you bleed out on their chitinous exoskeletons.

  The device given to them by Magnus Kane will be a one-time-use “Death Gas” weapon against the Gnawing Ones if they set it off in a cave on Saturday. If anyone remains in the cave after the Fumigation Device is set off, they will be struck with a “Death” effect after one minute if they stay in the caves. The device should be detonated after they leave the banners in front of the doors to trap the Gnawing Ones in the caves while the fumigation kills them. The gas will remain for one hour after it is set off, so the players need to loot before they activate the device, or they will have only 60 seconds to loot and run!
