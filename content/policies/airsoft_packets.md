Q: Can I use my bird seed packets?

A: No. Bird seed packets are not allowed at our campsite. We ask that players make packets containing biodegradable airsoft pellets. The packets must still fit into a 35mm film canister and be loosely packed. We recommend 6 mm pellets weighing .12 g, with about 1/2 tbsp. in each packet. Player packets will be checked for safety at events, along with weapon checks.
