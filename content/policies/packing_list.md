A non-comprehensive list of things to help you make sure you're bringing everything you need for game this weekend.

- **Base Clothes & Weather Clothes** - It's getting down to 43-47 degrees at night, so make sure to pack long-sleeve / long-legged black pants and shirts, as well as an alternative short-sleeve shirt to wear under your costuming during the day when temperatures rise to around 70 degrees. Bring a hat for the cold nights.
- **Footwear** - Try to wear boots or plain sneakers, but Somerset is notoriously muddy, so safety comes before style. Bring a few extra pairs of socks.
- **Costuming** - Including pouches, belts, weapons, shields, packets, cantrip tomes, and a personal light spell.

## Camping / Sleeping Gear

Pack a set of sleeping clothes, plus your toiletries, baby wipes, bug spray, sleeping bag / blankets, and a pillow. Strongly recommended:

- Toothbrush
- Toothpaste
- Hairbrush
- Deodorant
- Body wash
- Towel
- Soap
- Toilet paper
- Earplugs

**Bottled water** - Somerset groundwater is notoriously filthy without additional filtration.

**Snacks**.

**Sunscreen** if you're sensitive to UV.

**Cash** (this is a cash-only event).
