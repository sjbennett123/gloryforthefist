**Authors**: Ian Petley, Jerry Boyd, and Nick Denny

**Approved by**: Joseph Valenti

**Objective**: The Slash Five playtest is intended to streamline the combat math used for weapon combat. The goal is to simplify attack tracking, reduce bookkeeping during play, and make combat easier to read and resolve more quickly while preserving the tactical depth of the system.

**Summary**: This playtest introduces a simplified method for tracking attack resources and weapon-based abilities. Rather than requiring complex and time-consuming calculations, the system reduces clutter and makes it easier for players and staff to resolve combat consistently. It strengthens the core combat loop while keeping weapon proficiencies, backstabs, and special attack options manageable and easy to understand.

This playtest is meant to work alongside other combat adjustments and is designed to support a faster and more intuitive play experience. The system retains the fundamental structure of NERO weapon combat while simplifying the math so that characters can focus more on tactics and less on tracking repeated calculations.

The overall intent is to make combat decisions easier to understand, easier to teach, and easier to run at events without removing the strategic choices that make weapon fighting engaging. In practice, this means fewer steps between choosing an attack and resolving it, while still allowing players to build characters with distinct combat styles.
