**Updated**: April 17, 2018

**Version**: v1.0

**Authors**: Ian Petley, Jerry Boyd and Nick Denny

**Approved by**: Joseph Valenti

**Objective**:
To reduce the tag requirements for PCs who use archery.

**Summary**:
To use a bow or crossbow, arrow and bolt tags are no longer required. As this is a playtest and not a rules change, arrow and bolt tags may still be used; however, they now grant the weapon +1 damage when used.

Note: The number of arrow packets you have at the start of a module is the maximum number of arrows you can use during that module, regardless of whether you have tags. This playtest does not grant archers unlimited ammunition.
