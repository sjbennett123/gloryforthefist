**Version**: 1.0

**Updated**: April 17, 2018

**Authors**: Ian Petley, Jerry Boyd, and Nick Denny

**Approved By**: Joseph Valenti

**Objective**: Currently, it requires sixty seconds to refit physical armor (roleplaying tightening straps, repositioning plates), sixty seconds to refit dexterity armor (roleplaying stretching and/or shaking out tightened muscles), and an additional sixty seconds to refit arcane armor (roleplaying concentrating). The objective of this playtest is to unify the refitting process of physical armor, dexterity armor, and arcane armor into a single sixty-second action.

**Summary**: Under this playtest, players will be able to refit all their worn armor at the same time, allocating an equivalent portion of the roleplaying time to each type. For example, if a player was wearing both physical and dexterity armor, they would spend thirty seconds roleplaying the tightening of straps and then spend the remaining thirty seconds stretching. All other rules for refitting each individual type of armor must still be met (must be able to move for dexterity, no use of other in-game skills, no walking around while refitting physical armor, etc.).
