**Version**: 1.0

**Updated**: April 17, 2018

**Authors**: Ian Petley, Jerry Boyd, and Nick Denny

**Approved By**: Joseph Valenti

**Objective**: Currently, refitting physical armor requires sixty seconds (roleplaying tightening straps and repositioning plates), refitting dexterity armor requires sixty seconds (roleplaying stretching and/or shaking out tightened muscles), and refitting arcane armor requires an additional sixty seconds (roleplaying concentrating). The objective of this playtest is to unify the refitting process for physical armor, dexterity armor, and arcane armor into a single sixty-second action.

**Summary**: Under this playtest, players will be able to refit all of their worn armor at the same time, allocating an equal portion of the roleplaying time to each type. For example, if a player were wearing both physical and dexterity armor, they would spend thirty seconds roleplaying the tightening of straps and then spend the remaining thirty seconds stretching. All other rules for refitting each type of armor must still be followed (the player must be able to move while refitting dexterity armor, must not use other in-game skills, and must not walk around while refitting physical armor).
