Each NERO chapter abides by the rules listed in this book. Additionally, these rules can be modified, and new rules can be added through official NERO playtests. The NERO International Office makes an official list of rules being playtested available on its website at <www.nerolarp.com>. Each chapter must make a list of the playtests it is using available so that players attending events at that chapter can learn in advance which playtests will be in use.

## In use at Polar

- Properties (estates)
- Workshops
- Foam Weapon
- PvE Celestial
- Unified refitting
