**Updated**: August 1, 2014

**Version**: 1.1

**Authors**: Jason Mote and Jerry Boyd

**Effective Dates**: August 1, 2014, through December 31, 2015. This is an optional playtest for local NERO chapters and is not mandatory.

**Foreword**: This playtest is an attempt to incentivize better-looking weapons and costuming. Latex and Plastidip weapons were already in use before this playtest. NERO has been asking players and staff to reach the “next level” of theatrical costuming for many years. This playtest alone will not address this, but it will hopefully start to drive movement forward.

**Objective**: Latex weapons are great-looking visual representations of weapons and add quality to a player’s costuming. Coupled with a player’s costume, the result is an overall improvement in the game atmosphere for all players and deeper immersion in the game.

**Requirements**: Players are required to be in full costume before they may benefit from the playtest. The costume must be good enough to be awarded the Costume Bonus (NERO Rulebook, 9th ed., pp. 105–106). Simply owning a Latex or Plastidip weapon is not sufficient to receive the weapon bonus.

**Summary**: Latex weapons gain a costume bonus, similar to the costume bonus awarded for armor points. The Weapon Costume Bonus increases the base damage of the weapon. The increase in damage is determined by the weapon type:

- Small weapons and throwing daggers: +1 base damage (base 2 instead of base 1)
- Short/long one-handed weapons and staff weapons: +1 base damage (base 3 instead of base 2)
- Bow/crossbow: +2 base damage is applied to the arrow (base 5 instead of base 3)
- Two-handed weapons: +2 base damage (base 5 instead of base 3)

All manufactured Latex weapons must conform to the TOTAL LENGTH requirements listed in Table 7-7 of the NERO Rulebook, 9th ed. (p. 108). All weapons must be approved and pass safety inspection at each event at which they are used.

**Plastidip Inclusion**: Players may also choose to construct Plastidip sculpted-style weapons. These weapons must meet the following conditions to qualify for this playtest:

- Must follow the NERO rules for weapon construction (NERO Rulebook, 9th ed., pp. 107–109).
- Be of high quality and well constructed.
- Look great and be period-appropriate.
- Follow all weapon safety rules.

**Exclusions**: “Buffer”-style weapons are excluded from this playtest. This includes any weapon constructed with duct tape or kite tape, “ultra-light” buffer weapons, or “flatblade” buffer weapons. Javelins are also excluded from this playtest. There are no known safe javelins made of Latex or Plastidip.

**Playtest Challenges**: A list of “NERO Approved Latex Weapons” is currently under development. In addition, a policy outlining the standard methods for constructing NERO-safe Plastidip weapons is currently under development.
